Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

Welcome to Dudo. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice or our practices with regard to your personal info, please contact our support desk.

This Privacy Policy governs the privacy practices of our mobile application. It explains how we collect, safeguard, and disclose information that results from your use of our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal data that you voluntarily provide to us when registering on the application or interacting with platform features. This includes:

  • Account Metadata: Full Name, Registered Mobile Number, and Registration identification details.
  • Reward Delivery Details: Virtual IDs or reference identifiers provided explicitly during reward redemptions.
  • Profile Information: Email addresses and basic demographics when users update their profile dashboard.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your information for purposes based on legitimate business interests, the fulfillment of our contract with you, and compliance with operational requirements. We use the information to:

  • Facilitate account creation, user authentication, and secure logins.
  • Process reward payouts, user activity logs, and manage live dashboard metrics via secure real-time servers.
  • Request feedback, implement support channels, and manage platform integrity by identifying invalid submissions.

3. Third-Party Disclosures & Trackers

To keep the platform optimized, secure, and structurally scalable for future features, we cooperate with third-party service providers. Our platform integrates:

A. Firebase Backend SDK Infrastructure

We utilize encrypted Firebase Realtime Database clusters to manage instantly synchronized user metadata assets, reward configurations, and live account metrics securely.

B. Third-Party Monetization Frameworks

The app displays targeted static banner utilities powered by verified advertising partners. These networks might parse non-sensitive browser identifiers, cache parameters, or network logs to supply valid programmatic advertising outputs.

C. Offerwalls & Task Engines

External survey configurations or third-party task platforms (such as CPX Research, Bitlabs, and Timewall) process tracking identifiers using automated postback scripts to credit activity milestones safely into our database nodes.

4. Data Security & Safety Policies

We implement a variety of premium standard administrative and technical security measures to maintain the safety of your personal data. Your account nodes, activity states, and authentication credentials are fully encrypted inside database protocols. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or mobile storage is 100% secure.

5. Account Security & Fair Usage

To preserve the absolute safety of our platform ecosystem, users are expected to maintain fair usage. Submitting invalid transaction data or utilizing automated scripts will trigger security protocols, leading to immediate account restrictions to protect our genuine users and advertising partners.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services do not directly address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our database structure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any structural modifications by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. You are advised to review this page periodically for changes.

8. Contact Us

If you have any compliance questions or structural inquiries regarding this policy execution, you can connect directly with our support help desk operators via the support section inside the application interface.
