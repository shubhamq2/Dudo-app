Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to Dudo. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and your rights in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a seamless and rewarding experience, we collect specific data points when you use our application:

  • Personal Information: Full Name, Registered Mobile Number, and Email Address provided during registration.
  • Payment/Reward Details: Virtual IDs (e.g., Gaming IDs, UPI IDs) provided explicitly by you during reward redemptions.
  • Device & Advertising Data: IP address, Device OS, Advertising IDs (AAID), and network activity. This is strictly used for fraud prevention and ad-tracking mechanisms by our partners.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate business purposes and application functionality:

  • To create, manage, and authenticate your account securely.
  • To process reward payouts and maintain an accurate ledger of your earned coins.
  • To deliver personalized programmatic advertisements and tasks.
  • To prevent fraudulent activities, cheating, and multiple account abuses.

3. Third-Party Services & Trackers

We utilize trusted third-party SDKs to keep the platform optimized, secure, and operational. These services operate under their own strict privacy policies:

  • Backend Infrastructure: Google Firebase & Node.js API clusters for secure, encrypted real-time data syncing.
  • Advertising Partners: Networks like Adsterra, Monetag, etc., which may use Advertising IDs to serve relevant ads.
  • Offerwalls & Surveys: Partners like CPX Research, Bitlabs, and Torox process your activity to credit rewards to your account automatically via secure server-to-server postbacks.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

We retain your personal information only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the services.

Right to Delete Data: You have the absolute right to request the deletion of your account and associated personal data.

To delete your data, you can navigate to the Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account section within the app, or contact our support team directly. Upon request, your profile, balances, and history will be permanently erased from our servers within 30 days.

5. Data Security

We implement premium administrative and technical security measures (such as SSL encryption and secure API tokens) to maintain the safety of your personal data. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Our services do not directly target anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our database.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding your data and this Privacy Policy, please contact our support desk through the "Help & Support" section inside the application.
