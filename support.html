Frequently Asked Questions
1. How long does the payment take?

After submitting a withdrawal request, the money is transferred to your UPI account within 2 to 24 hours.

2. Why is Add Money showing pending?

For security reasons, the admin manually verifies the UTR. This can take 10 to 30 minutes.

3. How do I get the referral bonus?

When your friend creates a new account using your code, you instantly get ₹50 and your friend gets ₹10 bonus.

4. Why does a transaction get declined?

Requests are declined if an incorrect 12-digit UTR number is entered or if the submission is non-compliant.
