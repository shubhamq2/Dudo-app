Frequently Asked Questions
1. How long does it take to receive my reward?

After submitting a redeem request, your reward is processed within 2 to 24 hours.

2. Why is my task verification pending?

For security reasons, some tasks are manually verified to ensure fair play. This can take a few minutes.

3. How do I get the referral bonus?

When your friend creates an account using your code and remains active for 5 days, you receive your full referral points.

4. Why was my redeem request declined?

Requests are declined if an incorrect ID/Email is entered, or if fraudulent task completions are detected.
